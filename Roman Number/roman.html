Rule #1: Add!

When a smaller numeral comes after a bigger numeral, we add them.

VI

V = 5

I = 1

5 + 1 = 6
XI

X = 10

I = 1

10 + 1 = 11
🧠 Remember:
Smaller AFTER bigger → ADD ➕
➖

Rule #2: Subtract!

When a smaller numeral comes before a bigger numeral, we subtract it.

IV

I = 1

V = 5

5 − 1 = 4
IX

I = 1

X = 10

10 − 1 = 9
🧠 Remember:
Smaller BEFORE bigger → SUBTRACT ➖
